❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔹 What is keyword density?
Keyword density shows how often a word appears in your text, expressed as a percentage. For SEO, aim for 2-3% for your main keywords.
🔹 How is reading time calculated?
Based on average reading speed of 200-250 words per minute. Speaking time uses 150 words per minute.
🔹 Is it completely free?
Yes, 100% free with unlimited usage. No signup, no watermarks, no hidden fees.
🔹 Does it upload my text to a server?

🔹 What is the maximum text length?

🔹 Can I copy or clear the text easily?

🔹 Does it work on mobile devices?
Yes! The interface is fully responsive and works on all devices.

💡 Expert Tips for Best Results

For SEO, aim for 300+ words per page and keep your main keyword density between 2% and 3%. Use shorter sentences and paragraphs for better readability. The reading time estimate helps you match your content to your audience’s attention span.

  • 🎯 Word count – 300–1000 words is ideal for blog posts
  • 📏 Sentence length – keep average sentence length under 20 words
  • 🔑 Keyword density – avoid over‑optimisation (more than 5%)
  • ⚡ Readability – shorter paragraphs improve skimmability
  • 📄 Unlimited text – write without worrying about limits
