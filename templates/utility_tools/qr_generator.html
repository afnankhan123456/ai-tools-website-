💡 Expert Tips for Best Results

For high contrast, keep the foreground dark and background light. Use rounded or circle shapes for a modern look. Test your QR code with a scanner before using it publicly. For WiFi QR codes, double‑check the password and encryption type to ensure automatic connection.

  • 🎯 Contrast matters – dark foreground on light background scans best
  • 📏 Size accordingly – larger QR codes are easier to scan from a distance
  • 🖼️ PNG download – lossless format for high-quality printing
  • ⚡ Custom shapes – make your QR code stand out while staying scannable
